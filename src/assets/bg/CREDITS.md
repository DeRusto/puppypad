# Background tiles

Every tile here was drawn for PuppyPad by hand as SVG in this repository (the see-through ones redraw
patterns the site and starter layouts already use, so they pick up the member's page colour), and is released as
CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), so members
may use them on their sites and anyone may copy them. The 88×31 buttons and stamps in
`../buttons/` and the effects in `../fx/` are made and licensed the same way.
